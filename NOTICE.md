# Provenance and redistribution

This is a research handoff. No new open-source license is assigned by this migration. Upstream benchmark tasks, generated patches/tool output and vendored historical source retain their original terms. Models and benchmark/environment payloads are not redistributed here. Historical third-party harness excerpts are retained in the HF provenance layer for audit.

The portable scheduling code was adapted from the project's analysis scripts; originals are retained under provenance/original-analysis. The original cluster paths in those files are provenance only. Authentication data, signed upload URLs and unrelated Codex conversations are excluded.
